What GEO actually is

Generative Engine Optimization is the practice of making your brand visible inside AI-generated answers. Where SEO optimizes for a ranked list of links, GEO optimizes for being named and cited in a synthesised response.

The mechanics are different. A language model doesn't keep a ranked index — it draws on what it learned during training plus what it retrieves live at answer time. So getting recommended depends on three things being true at once:

  • The engine can understand you. Your site states plainly what you offer, to whom, and where. Vague brand poetry is invisible to a machine.
  • The engine can verify you. Your business details are consistent across your site, Google Business Profile, directories and social platforms — that consistency is how an entity gets established.
  • The engine can quote you. Your content contains specific, self-contained, factual passages worth citing.

Why this matters now

Every query answered by an AI assistant is a query that may never reach your website through a traditional search result. Brands that establish themselves in these systems early gain a compounding advantage — models and their retrieval sources reinforce what already appears authoritative. Late movers are competing against incumbency.

GEO, AEO and SEO together

GEO doesn't replace search fundamentals. AI retrieval leans heavily on pages that already rank, so strong SEO is the entry ticket. AEO then structures your content as direct answers, and GEO builds the entity and trust signals that get you named. We run all three as one program — see the combined overview.

GEO questions

GEO is the practice of optimizing your brand and content so generative AI engines — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google's AI Overviews — understand, trust and recommend your business in the answers they generate for users.

No one can force an AI recommendation, and any agency claiming otherwise is overselling. What we do is systematically build the signals these engines demonstrably rely on: entity clarity, structured data, citable content and credible third-party mentions. We also measure what AI engines say about you before and during the engagement, so progress is visible.

SEO optimizes for position in a ranked list of links. GEO optimizes for inclusion in a generated answer. They share foundations — good content, technical health, authority — but GEO adds entity consistency, schema depth and content formatted to be quoted rather than clicked.

We track branded and category prompts across the major AI engines on a regular schedule, recording whether and how your brand appears. Combined with referral traffic from AI sources and overall lead volume, that gives a practical picture of AI visibility over time.

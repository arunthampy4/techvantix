The zero-click reality

A large share of searches now end on the results page itself: a featured snippet answers the question, a voice assistant reads it aloud, or an AI Overview summarises it. For businesses, this is either a threat or an opportunity depending on one thing — whose content gets used as the answer.

Answer Engine Optimization is the discipline of winning that position. Even when it produces no click, being the cited answer builds brand recognition and authority at the exact moment of research.

What we change on your pages

  • Structure by question. Clear headings phrased as the questions people actually ask, one topic per section, rather than long undifferentiated paragraphs.
  • Answer in the first 40–60 words. Snippets are extracted from concise, complete passages. Elaborate after you've answered, not before.
  • Mark it up. FAQPage and HowTo schema so engines can identify question-answer pairs unambiguously.
  • Match the format to the query. Definition queries want a paragraph; process queries want a numbered list; comparison queries want a table. Format mismatches lose snippets.
  • Cover the follow-ups. People Also Ask questions are a map of what users wonder next — answering them keeps you in the conversation.

Where AEO fits

AEO is the most immediately actionable of the three search disciplines — restructuring existing content can win snippets in weeks. It pairs naturally with SEO, which gets your pages eligible in the first place, and GEO, which builds the entity trust that AI engines weigh when deciding whom to cite.

FAQ

AEO questions

AEO is the practice of structuring content so search and answer engines select it as the direct answer to a question — in featured snippets, People Also Ask boxes, voice assistant responses and AI-generated overviews.

They overlap but differ in focus. AEO is about formatting content as extractable answers to specific questions. GEO is about establishing your brand as a trusted entity that generative engines name and recommend. Most programs benefit from both.

Faster than most SEO work. Because it often means restructuring content that already ranks, featured snippet wins can appear within weeks of implementation.

Sometimes a snippet answers the question completely and the user doesn't click — but you gain prominent brand visibility at the moment of research, and snippets sit above all other results. For most businesses the authority and recognition outweigh the lost click, and many snippets still drive strong click-through.

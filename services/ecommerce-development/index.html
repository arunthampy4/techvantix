Choosing the right platform

We don't push one platform for every project. Shopify suits most businesses: it handles hosting, security, updates and checkout reliably, launches in weeks, and your team can run it from a phone. WooCommerce makes sense when you're already invested in WordPress and need deep content integration. A custom build is warranted when your pricing rules, catalogue structure or back-office integrations genuinely exceed what platforms handle.

The recommendation comes out of your requirements, not our preference — and we'll tell you honestly when the cheaper option is the right one.

The details that decide revenue

  • Speed. Conversion rate falls measurably with every extra second of load time. We optimize images, limit app bloat and keep the theme lean.
  • Trust. Clear pricing, honest delivery estimates, visible return policies, reviews and professional design — shoppers abandon stores that feel risky.
  • Frictionless checkout. Guest checkout, minimal fields, familiar payment methods, no surprise costs at the final step.
  • Marketing-ready from day one. Analytics, ad pixels, product feeds for Google and Meta, and abandoned-cart recovery configured before launch, not bolted on later.

Launch is the start

A store with no traffic makes no sales. We also run the demand that fills it — Google Shopping, Meta campaigns, product SEO — and the WhatsApp automation that recovers abandoned carts and answers buyer questions instantly.

Why SEO alone is no longer enough

For two decades, being on page one of Google was the whole game. That game has changed. Today your customers ask ChatGPT for recommendations, read Google's AI Overviews instead of clicking through, and speak questions to voice assistants. If your brand isn't part of those AI-generated answers, you're invisible to a fast-growing share of your market — even with decent rankings.

That's why every TechVantix search program covers three disciplines working together:

  • SEO — the fundamentals done properly: technical health, content depth, internal linking and authority, so Google ranks you for the keywords that actually convert.
  • GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) — optimizing how AI models perceive your brand: consistent entity signals, structured data, citable content and third-party mentions that make generative engines name you in their answers.
  • AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) — structuring content as direct, complete answers: FAQ and How-To schema, snippet-friendly formatting and question-led pages that win featured snippets and voice results.

What working with us looks like

We start with a deep audit of your website, rankings, competitors and current AI visibility — including checking what ChatGPT and Google's AI actually say about your category today. From there you get a prioritised roadmap: quick technical wins first, then a content and authority engine that compounds month after month.

You'll never wonder what you're paying for. Monthly reports show rankings, organic traffic, AI citations and — most importantly — the leads and revenue search is generating for your business.

FAQ

SEO, GEO & AEO questions

SEO optimizes your website to rank in traditional search engine results. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) optimizes your brand to be cited and recommended by AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) structures your content to be chosen as the direct answer in featured snippets, voice search and AI Overviews. Modern visibility requires all three, and our programs combine them.

AI models recommend brands they can understand and trust. We build that trust through consistent entity signals (structured data, clear service descriptions), authoritative content that answers real questions, and credible third-party mentions. Over time, this makes generative engines more likely to cite your brand when users ask for recommendations in your category.

Technical fixes often produce movement within 6–12 weeks. Meaningful ranking, traffic and lead growth typically builds over 3–6 months, depending on your market's competitiveness and your site's starting point. We set milestone expectations upfront and report progress monthly.

No honest agency can guarantee a specific ranking — search engines don't sell positions. What we do guarantee is a transparent, proven process: clear KPIs, monthly reporting, and strategy adjustments based on real data until the results compound.

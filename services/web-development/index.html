A beautiful website that doesn't convert is just art

Plenty of agencies can make something pretty. We build websites as growth assets: every page has a job, every section moves the visitor toward an action, and everything is measured. Design, copy structure, load speed and SEO are engineered together — because they only work together.

What every TechVantix build includes

  • Mobile-first, responsive design — most of your visitors are on a phone; your site is designed for them first, then scaled up beautifully.
  • Performance engineering — fast hosting guidance, optimized assets and clean code targeting excellent Core Web Vitals scores.
  • On-page SEO foundations — semantic HTML, schema markup, sitemaps, and metadata so Google (and AI crawlers) understand every page.
  • Conversion elements — clear calls-to-action, trust signals, enquiry forms and optional AI chat, positioned where they perform.
  • Analytics & tracking — know exactly where visitors come from and what they do, from day one.
  • Easy content management — update your own content without calling a developer.

Redesigns without the SEO horror stories

Redesigning an existing site? Done wrong, a relaunch can wipe out years of SEO equity overnight. We handle migrations properly — URL mapping, redirects, content preservation and pre/post-launch checks — so you get a better website and keep your rankings.

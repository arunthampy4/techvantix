Leads are a system, not a stroke of luck

Businesses that grow predictably don't have better luck — they have a system: a compelling offer, traffic aimed at the right people, a page built to convert, and follow-up that never sleeps. We build that system for you, run it, and improve it every month.

The anatomy of a TechVantix lead engine

  • The offer — a free audit, consultation, quote or resource your ideal customer genuinely wants. This is where most campaigns are won or lost.
  • The traffic — search ads for high-intent buyers, social ads to reach people who don't know you yet, and retargeting to recover the ones who almost converted.
  • The landing page — focused, fast and persuasive, with trust signals and a friction-free form. Continuously A/B tested.
  • The follow-up — instant AI-powered responses, automated nurture sequences and sales-ready handoffs. Speed-to-lead is the highest-leverage factor in conversion, so we automate it.
  • The measurement — every lead tracked from source to outcome in your CRM, so budget flows to the channels producing customers, not just contacts.

Quality over quantity

A hundred junk leads cost more than they're worth. Our campaigns qualify prospects on the way in — through targeting, messaging and smart form design — and our scoring flags the hottest leads for immediate attention. Your team spends time on conversations that can close.
